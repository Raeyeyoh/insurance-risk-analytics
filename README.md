Insurance Risk Analytics

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing historical car insurance data for AlphaCare Insurance Solutions (ACIS). The objective is to identify risk patterns, profitability trends, and potential drivers of insurance claims using exploratory data analysis, statistical methods, and reproducible data pipelines.

The project supports business decisions related to:

- Risk-based pricing
- Customer segmentation
- Marketing optimization
- Claim prediction
- Portfolio profitability analysis

Business Objective

AlphaCare Insurance Solutions aims to improve its pricing strategy and marketing effectiveness using data-driven insights from historical insurance claim data.

The analysis helps identify:

- High-risk and low-risk customer groups
- Geographic claim patterns
- Vehicle types associated with higher losses
- Profitability trends using Loss Ratio and Margin

Dataset Overview

The dataset contains:

- Policy information
- Client demographics
- Vehicle details
- Insurance plan information
- Premium and claim data
